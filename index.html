Artist Club • Est. 2014

Where bold ideas, vibrant palettes, and fearless artists meet.

Nova Prism Collective is a community-driven artist club based in your city. We host collaborative studio nights, critique circles, public murals, and immersive showcases that spotlight experimental work.

We’re here to nurture brave creativity.

The club brings together painters, illustrators, sculptors, poets, musicians, and interdisciplinary makers. Weekly studio sessions, community critiques, and collaborative installations help members grow their practice with honest feedback and playful experimentation.
